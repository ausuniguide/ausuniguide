Common questions

You probably want to know…

How much does it cost to study in Australia?
Tuition typically ranges from AUD $30,000 to AUD $52,000 per year for international students, depending on the university and course. Add roughly AUD $24,505 per year for living costs (the official visa minimum), and AUD $500–700 for mandatory OSHC health cover. Sydney and Melbourne sit at the higher end; Adelaide, Brisbane, and Perth are notably cheaper.
How long does the student visa take?
Subclass 500 processing usually takes 4–12 weeks. Apply at least three months before your course start date. Submit a complete application with proper financial documents, a strong Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) statement, and health checks done early to avoid delays. We always recommend working with a MARA-registered Migration Agent.
What IELTS score do I need?
Undergraduate programs typically require IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall. Masters programs need 6.5–7.0. Medicine, Law, and Teaching usually require 7.0+ with strong individual band scores. TOEFL iBT and PTE Academic are accepted equivalents. AusUni Guide's eligibility checker shows the exact requirement for each course.
Can I work while studying?
Yes. The Subclass 500 visa allows 48 hours of work per fortnight during semester, and unlimited hours during official course breaks. You'll need a Tax File Number (TFN) — apply free at ato.gov.au within four weeks of arriving. Minimum wage in Australia is currently AUD $24.10/hour.
Is ARIA available for free users?
Free users get the full ARIA Knowledge Base — 52 pre-written expert answers covering the most common student questions. The Premium AI Chat unlocks unlimited personalised conversations with Claude AI, trained specifically on the Australian student journey.
Do I keep my data if I cancel Premium?
Yes. Your application progress, checklist completions, and university shortlist all stay with your account. You'll lose access to ARIA AI chat, comparison tools, and the eligibility checker, but everything else — including the FAQ knowledge base — remains free forever.
Is this an official Australian Government app?
No. AusUni Guide is an independent app built to help international students navigate Australian universities. We are not affiliated with the Australian Government, the Department of Home Affairs, or any specific university. For official visa matters, always consult immi.homeaffairs.gov.au or a MARA-registered Migration Agent.
