Quick help

Common questions we get — you might find your answer here first.

ARIA isn't responding

Check your internet connection. Free users get the FAQ knowledge base; ARIA AI chat requires a Premium subscription.

Premium isn't unlocking

Restore purchases from Settings, or wait a few minutes for Google Play to sync. Still stuck? Email us with your purchase ID.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AusUni Guide → Cancel. Done.

I want a refund

Refunds are processed by Google Play directly. Request via play.google.com/store/account/orders within 48 hours of purchase.

The app crashed

Force-close the app and reopen it. If it persists, email us your device model and what you were doing when it crashed.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Email us from your registered address and we'll remove your account and all associated data within 14 days.

I have a feature request

We love these. Email us — every Premium subscriber's request gets read and considered for the roadmap.

Visa or migration question?

We can't give individual visa advice. Please consult a MARA-registered Migration Agent or visit immi.homeaffairs.gov.au for official guidance.
