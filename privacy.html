Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026 · Effective immediately

Summary: We collect the minimum information needed to make the app work. We don't sell your data. ARIA AI conversations are processed through Anthropic's Claude API but never stored on our servers. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

AusUni Guide ("we", "our", "us") is an independent mobile application owned and operated by Mustansir Yaqub, based in Melbourne, Australia. We are not affiliated with the Australian Government, the Department of Home Affairs, or any specific Australian university.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name and email address when you create a free account
  • Profile preferences: study area, GPA, English test scores (only if you choose to use the eligibility checker)
  • Checklist progress and saved universities

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier (anonymous UUID) to enforce free-tier daily limits
  • App usage analytics (anonymised, via standard Android frameworks)
  • Crash reports to help us fix bugs

Subscription information

Premium subscriptions are processed by RevenueCat and Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details — only your subscription status (active or inactive).

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the core features of the app (course search, comparison, checklist)
  • To deliver ARIA AI conversations to Premium users
  • To enforce daily message limits for free users
  • To send important account or service updates (rare)
  • To diagnose bugs and improve the app

4. ARIA AI and your conversations

When you send a message to ARIA, the message and recent conversation history are sent to Anthropic's Claude API via our Supabase backend. These conversations are:

  • Not stored on our servers after the response is generated
  • Used only to generate ARIA's response to you
  • Subject to Anthropic's privacy policy during processing

Do not share sensitive personal information (passport numbers, financial details, etc.) in your ARIA conversations.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with the service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Supabase — backend database and edge functions hosting
  • Anthropic — Claude AI processing for ARIA conversations (Premium only)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Google Play — payment processing and app distribution

6. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), Supabase Row Level Security policies, and secrets management for API keys. However, no system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password and never share your account credentials.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at supportmarketiq@gmail.com. We respond within 14 days.

8. Children's privacy

AusUni Guide is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Cookies and tracking

The mobile app does not use traditional cookies. We use standard Android device identifiers and locally stored preferences (AsyncStorage) to remember your settings between sessions. This data stays on your device.

10. International users

AusUni Guide is used by international students worldwide. Your data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate (Australia, United States). By using the app you consent to this transfer. We comply with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
