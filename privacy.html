This Privacy Policy explains how MustiIQ ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the mobile application UniPath Australia (package name com.mustansir.yaqub.ausuniguide), distributed through the Google Play Store.

In short: We collect the minimum information needed to make the app work. We do not sell your data. ARIA AI conversations are processed through Anthropic's Claude API but are not stored on our servers. You can delete your account at any time.

1. About the app

UniPath Australia is an independent informational application that helps international students research Australian universities, courses, scholarships and the student visa process. It is developed and operated by MustiIQ in Melbourne, Australia.

UniPath Australia is not affiliated with the Australian Government, the Department of Home Affairs, or any Australian university.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, email address and password, if you choose to create a free account
  • Optional profile details: study area, GPA, English test scores — only if you use the course eligibility checker
  • Checklist progress and saved universities

You may also use UniPath Australia as a guest without creating an account. In guest mode we do not collect a name, email address or password.

Information collected automatically

  • An anonymous device identifier, generated on first launch, used to apply daily usage limits to the ARIA assistant
  • Standard Android crash reports and basic usage diagnostics

Subscription information

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment card details. We receive only your subscription status (active or inactive) and an anonymised subscriber identifier.

3. Where your data is stored

Account details, passwords and checklist progress are stored locally on your device using Android's app storage. They are not uploaded to our servers and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's core features — course search, university comparison, checklists
  • To deliver ARIA AI responses to premium subscribers
  • To apply fair-use limits on the ARIA assistant
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app
  • To respond when you contact support

5. ARIA AI and your conversations

When a premium subscriber sends a message to ARIA, that message and recent conversation context are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API through a Supabase Edge Function. These conversations:

  • Are not retained on our servers after the response is returned
  • Are used solely to generate a reply to you
  • Are subject to Anthropic's privacy policy during processing

Please do not share sensitive personal information such as passport numbers or financial details in ARIA conversations.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share data only with the service providers required to operate the app:

  • Supabase Inc. — backend hosting and edge functions
  • Anthropic PBC — Claude AI processing for ARIA (premium subscribers only)
  • RevenueCat Inc. — subscription management
  • Google LLC — app distribution and payment processing via Google Play

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all network requests, Supabase Row Level Security policies, and server-side secrets management for API keys. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and do not share your account credentials.

8. Your rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and its data — clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes everything stored locally
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the app

To exercise any of these rights, email supportmarketiq@gmail.com. We respond within 14 days.

9. Children's privacy

UniPath Australia is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

UniPath Australia is used by students worldwide. Your data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, including Australia and the United States. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
