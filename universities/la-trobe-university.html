Common questions

How much does La Trobe University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at La Trobe University is A$43,000 for undergraduate courses and A$44,200 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at La Trobe University?

La Trobe University has 237 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (106 undergraduate, 98 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at La Trobe University?
