How much does University of Technology Sydney cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Technology Sydney is A$49,450 for undergraduate courses and A$50,465 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Technology Sydney?

University of Technology Sydney has 482 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (249 undergraduate, 189 postgraduate).
