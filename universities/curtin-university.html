How much does Curtin University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Curtin University is A$45,814 for undergraduate courses and A$44,980 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Curtin University?

Curtin University has 352 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (103 undergraduate, 152 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Curtin University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Science (Project Management) (104 weeks) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Curtin’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$19,500 per year.
