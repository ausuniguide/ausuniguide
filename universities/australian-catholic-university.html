Common questions

How much does Australian Catholic University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Australian Catholic University is A$35,624 for undergraduate courses and A$35,624 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Australian Catholic University?

Australian Catholic University has 143 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (103 undergraduate, 25 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Australian Catholic University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Clinical Exercise Physiology has the lowest estimated annual tuition among ACU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$24,054 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
