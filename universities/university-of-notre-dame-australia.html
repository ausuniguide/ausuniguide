Common questions

How much does University of Notre Dame Australia cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Notre Dame Australia is A$37,600 for undergraduate courses and A$36,401 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Notre Dame Australia?

University of Notre Dame Australia has 119 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (70 undergraduate, 21 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of Notre Dame Australia?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Business Administration has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Notre Dame’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$33,130 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
