Common questions

How much does CQUniversity cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at CQUniversity is A$36,720 for undergraduate courses and A$38,640 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at CQUniversity?

CQUniversity has 94 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (38 undergraduate, 41 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at CQUniversity?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Nutrition & Dietetics has the lowest estimated annual tuition among CQU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$24,000 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
