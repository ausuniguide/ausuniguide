Common questions

How much does Charles Darwin University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Charles Darwin University is A$31,733 for undergraduate courses and A$36,094 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Charles Darwin University?

Charles Darwin University has 146 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (53 undergraduate, 71 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Charles Darwin University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Teaching has the lowest estimated annual tuition among CDU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$28,104 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
