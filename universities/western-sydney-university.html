Common questions

How much does Western Sydney University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Western Sydney University is A$37,759 for undergraduate courses and A$39,336 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Western Sydney University?

Western Sydney University has 306 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (148 undergraduate, 125 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Western Sydney University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Creative Music Therapy has the lowest estimated annual tuition among WSU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$26,526 per year.
