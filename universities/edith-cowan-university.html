Common questions

How much does Edith Cowan University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Edith Cowan University is A$41,150 for undergraduate courses and A$41,250 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Edith Cowan University?

Edith Cowan University has 167 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (87 undergraduate, 65 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Edith Cowan University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Education has the lowest estimated annual tuition among ECU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$37,650 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
