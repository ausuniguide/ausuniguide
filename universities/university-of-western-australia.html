Common questions

How much does The University of Western Australia cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at The University of Western Australia is A$50,700 for undergraduate courses and A$51,750 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at The University of Western Australia?

The University of Western Australia has 311 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (121 undergraduate, 118 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at The University of Western Australia?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Education (Coursework and Dissertation) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UWA’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$39,300 per year.
