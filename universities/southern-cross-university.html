Common questions

How much does Southern Cross University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Southern Cross University is A$26,000 for undergraduate courses and A$26,000 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Southern Cross University?

Southern Cross University has 117 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (63 undergraduate, 33 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Southern Cross University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Business Administration has the lowest estimated annual tuition among SCU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$26,000 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
