Common questions

How much does University of New England cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of New England is A$31,412 for undergraduate courses and A$32,314 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of New England?

University of New England has 121 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (60 undergraduate, 33 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of New England?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Laws has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UNE’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$22,297 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
