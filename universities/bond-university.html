Common questions

How much does Bond University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Bond University is A$72,338 for undergraduate courses and A$54,829 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Bond University?

Bond University has 168 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (69 undergraduate, 84 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Bond University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of International Relations (Professional) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Bond’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$40,605 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
