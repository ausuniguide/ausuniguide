How much does Queensland University of Technology cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Queensland University of Technology is A$43,998 for undergraduate courses and A$45,300 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Queensland University of Technology?

Queensland University of Technology has 275 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (158 undergraduate, 103 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Queensland University of Technology?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Counselling has the lowest estimated annual tuition among QUT’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$28,050 per year.
