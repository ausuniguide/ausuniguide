Common questions

How much does University of the Sunshine Coast cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of the Sunshine Coast is A$29,500 for undergraduate courses and A$31,500 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of the Sunshine Coast?

University of the Sunshine Coast has 141 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (85 undergraduate, 34 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of the Sunshine Coast?

On the CRICOS register, Master of International Development has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UniSC’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$25,600 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
