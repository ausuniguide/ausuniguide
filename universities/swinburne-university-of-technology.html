How much does Swinburne University of Technology cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Swinburne University of Technology is A$43,900 for undergraduate courses and A$40,000 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Swinburne University of Technology?

Swinburne University of Technology has 359 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (182 undergraduate, 148 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Swinburne University of Technology?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Arts (Media and Communications) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Swinburne’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$24,500 per year.
