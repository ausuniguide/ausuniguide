Common questions

How much does Deakin University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Deakin University is A$44,430 for undergraduate courses and A$44,476 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Deakin University?

Deakin University has 230 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (112 undergraduate, 96 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Deakin University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Deakin’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$38,400 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
