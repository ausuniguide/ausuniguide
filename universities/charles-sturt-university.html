Common questions

How much does Charles Sturt University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Charles Sturt University is A$37,040 for undergraduate courses and A$35,000 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Charles Sturt University?

Charles Sturt University has 62 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (27 undergraduate, 23 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Charles Sturt University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Arts (Theological Studies) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among CSU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$24,560 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
