Common questions

How much does Torrens University Australia cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Torrens University Australia is A$35,000 for undergraduate courses and A$32,276 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Torrens University Australia?

Torrens University Australia has 153 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (59 undergraduate, 59 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Torrens University Australia?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Education (Inclusive Education) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among Torrens’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$25,193 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
