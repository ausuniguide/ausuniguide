Common questions

How much does University of Wollongong cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Wollongong is A$37,968 for undergraduate courses and A$37,776 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Wollongong?

University of Wollongong has 327 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (179 undergraduate, 111 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of Wollongong?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Teaching (Secondary) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UOW’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$29,616 per year.
