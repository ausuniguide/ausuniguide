Common questions

How much does University of Tasmania cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Tasmania is A$41,970 for undergraduate courses and A$41,974 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Tasmania?

University of Tasmania has 209 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (71 undergraduate, 55 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of Tasmania?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Business Administration (International) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UTAS’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$33,832 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
