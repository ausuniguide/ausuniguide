How much does Griffith University cost for international students?

How many courses can international students take at Griffith University?

Griffith University has 283 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (174 undergraduate, 98 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Griffith University?
