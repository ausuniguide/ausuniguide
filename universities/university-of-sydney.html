Common questions

How much does The University of Sydney cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at The University of Sydney is A$59,760 for undergraduate courses and A$54,900 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at The University of Sydney?

The University of Sydney has 595 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (173 undergraduate, 370 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at The University of Sydney?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Facilities Management has the lowest estimated annual tuition among USyd’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$36,000 per year.
