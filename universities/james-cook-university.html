Common questions

How much does James Cook University cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at James Cook University is A$42,477 for undergraduate courses and A$39,931 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at James Cook University?

James Cook University has 121 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (54 undergraduate, 36 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at James Cook University?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Human Services has the lowest estimated annual tuition among JCU’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$35,342 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
