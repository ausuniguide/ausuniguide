Common questions

How much does University of Southern Queensland cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Southern Queensland is A$33,600 for undergraduate courses and A$31,840 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Southern Queensland?

University of Southern Queensland has 59 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (38 undergraduate, 18 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of Southern Queensland?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Cyber Security (MCYS) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UniSQ’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$31,200 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
