Common questions

How much does University of Canberra cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at University of Canberra is A$38,000 for undergraduate courses and A$39,900 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at University of Canberra?

University of Canberra has 205 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (129 undergraduate, 56 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at University of Canberra?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Communication has the lowest estimated annual tuition among UC’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$33,000 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
