Common questions

How much does Federation University Australia cost for international students?

Based on the CRICOS register (September 2026), median annual tuition at Federation University Australia is A$34,200 for undergraduate courses and A$34,350 for postgraduate coursework. Fees vary by course — see the full list on this page.

How many courses can international students take at Federation University Australia?

Federation University Australia has 163 undergraduate and postgraduate coursework programs registered on CRICOS (76 undergraduate, 52 postgraduate).

What is the lowest-cost master’s at Federation University Australia?

On the CRICOS register, Master of Health (Research Practice) has the lowest estimated annual tuition among FedUni’s master’s programs of a year or longer, at about A$26,600 per year.

Source: CRICOS register (Australian Government, data.gov.au), September 2026. Tuition is the provider’s registered estimate for the full course; annual figure = total ÷ course length.
